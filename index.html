Texas Hold’em, one decision at a time.

Each player receives two private cards. Make the best five-card hand using any combination of your cards and the five community cards.

The four betting rounds

Preflop: two hole cards. Flop: three community cards. Turn: a fourth card. River: the fifth. Players act clockwise after the big blind preflop, and after the dealer on later streets.

Your choices

Check when nothing is owed. Call to match a bet. Raise to increase it. Fold to give up the hand. “Raise to” is your total contribution this round, not the amount added. No limit means you may bet your entire stack. All-in players remain eligible for the pots they contributed to.

What the coach means

Equity estimates your share of the pot at showdown against random hands, including ties. Pot odds are the call cost divided by the pot after your call. Draw outs here count unseen next cards that improve your hand category to a straight, flush, full house, quads or straight flush. They are not guaranteed winning cards and do not count every pair improvement.

Strong starting hands and good position help. Compare your equity with the price of a call, but consider opponents’ ranges and future bets. The coach does not model fold equity, implied odds, or advanced strategy.

Hand rankings, strongest first

Straight flush → Four of a kind → Full house → Flush → Straight → Three of a kind → Two pair → One pair → High card. An ace can be high or low in a straight.
